ZenBlocks: privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Español

ZenBlocks is a game by Binary Tinkerers.

We do not collect any personal data. The game has no accounts, ads or analytics, and it sends no information to any server, ours or anyone else's.

  • What is stored on your phone: your records, finished challenges, settings and the game in progress. They stay on the device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Purchases: the full game is bought through Google Play, which handles payment under its own privacy policy. The game only asks Google Play whether your account owns the purchase. We never see or store payment details.
  • Children: since we collect no data, we collect no data from children either.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.

Contact: juegos@binarytinkerers.com
