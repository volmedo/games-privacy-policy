Binary Tinkerers games privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Español

This policy applies to the games published by Binary Tinkerers: ZenBlocks. If a game ever handles data differently, it will get its own section here.

We do not collect any personal data. The games have no accounts, ads or analytics, and they send no information to any server, ours or anyone else's.

  • What is stored on your device: your progress, records, settings and the game in progress. They stay on the device and are deleted when you uninstall the game. We have no access to them.
  • Purchases: purchases go through the store you downloaded the game from (for example, Google Play), which handles payment under its own privacy policy (Google's). The game only asks the store whether your account owns the purchase. We never see or store payment details.
  • Children: since we collect no data, we collect no data from children either.
  • Deleting your data: everything stays on your device, so uninstalling the game deletes it.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
